## S1 · 0:00-3.5 · "…the harder it became to choose a path" · Captions Only
**Refs:** TXT-002 (hoofd), TXT-001

```
Hook. Three-register stack under the chin, like TXT-002: small white Montserrat "the harder it became to", then
a huge orange Playfair Italic "choose a path" (2.5-3x size, soft orange glow, tight leading so it interlocks),
then a widely tracked grey sub-line "THE DEEPER I GOT" tucked just under the descender of "path".
Motion: "THE DEEPER I GOT" fades in first on "deeper" (tracking 0.55em -> 0.3em), the white line rises word by
word, and "choose a path" lands as one unit with blur-to-sharp + scale 0.92 -> 1, resolving exactly on "path".
A thin orange hairline draws left to right under "choose a path" just after it lands (like a path being drawn).
Sound: one clean pop on the landing. Exit together with a quick fade when the sentence ends.
```
