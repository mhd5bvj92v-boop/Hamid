# JUST A TAP — locked 20s storyboard

**Format**: 1920x1080, 60 fps, 1200 frames. UI-first, rich minimalism, warm off-white and ink with orange accent. Fast, playful, controlled. Narrative is a visual metaphor for a familiar contactless interaction, not a technical claim about settlement or security. VO is optional and not assumed to exist.

## On-screen copy
Use very little large typography; keep most wording within actual UI components. Optional hero phrases: `READY TO PAY`, `€24.90`, `PAYMENT COMPLETE`, `CONTACTLESS`, `JUST A TAP.`. Avoid claiming a specific payment processing speed, security guarantee or technical mechanism.

## Sound direction
Soft digital tactile SFX, one crisp confirmation sound, subtle texture. No loud generic whoosh on every movement. Do not fabricate audio assets. If no supplied VO/music, render silent and provide a cue sheet.

## Important design benchmark
The first 5 seconds must show the difference between basic motion and premium UI motion: precise visual hierarchy, nuanced state changes, sophisticated stagger and a convincing shared-element card-to-wallet morph. It should look good as a screenshot, but better in motion.
